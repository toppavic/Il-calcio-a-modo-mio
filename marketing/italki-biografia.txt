Ciao! I'm Matteo, born in Florence and living in the Tuscan hills, near Florence.

Three days a week I work in a reception centre, where every day I speak with people who are learning Italian from scratch. That taught me the most important thing: speak slowly, use simple words, and never make anyone feel embarrassed for a mistake.

I have also been a football coach for twenty years, and I write: I published a book about a youth team that won its league without losing a single game. So if you love football, Tuscany, food or stories, we will never run out of things to talk about.

My lessons are relaxed conversations in real Italian, the one people speak at the bar, at the market and on the pitch. I adapt to your level: if you are a beginner we go step by step, if you already speak a little we work on fluency and confidence. I correct you gently and send you the new words after the lesson.
